## 1. Features and scope of use

The App is a personal creative information management tool. Its features include worlds, records and templates, attributes and text editing, relationship graphs, timelines, gallery categories and images, character profiles and settings, appearance parts and outfit customization, accessory visibility, illustrations, desktop characters and custom speech, data links, data package import and export, and optional iCloud sync. Available features depend on the installed version.

Core creative data is stored locally. You do not need to register an App account. iCloud sync uses the iCloud account currently signed in to macOS and requires the relevant system services, a network connection and available storage. The App does not currently provide a public community, collaboration, a content publishing platform or cloud-based generative AI. “Coming soon” messages in Home, Space and Performance do not mean those features are available or promise a release date.

The App currently supports Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15.6 or later. Display results may vary by system, device and assets. Requirements for later versions are subject to their release information.

### Free trial and one-time lifetime purchase

**Free trial.** The App offers a 14-day free trial of all features, claimed through an Apple in-app purchase. It runs for 14 × 24 consecutive hours from the original trial transaction time recorded by Apple, rather than counting actual usage time. Restoring purchases, reinstalling or changing devices does not restart or extend the same account’s trial. **The trial does not automatically charge you or convert into a paid subscription when it ends.**

**One-time purchase.** Continuing to use all features requires an actively confirmed lifetime purchase, available under Settings → Purchase. This is a non-consumable in-app purchase: one payment unlocks all App features, with no fixed access expiry and no monthly or yearly renewal. Price, currency and taxes are those shown in the purchase interface and Apple’s confirmation sheet; this agreement does not fix a price. Use remains subject to supported systems and devices, the software license and Apple service availability. Purchasing the App does not change iCloud storage or service rules.

**Expiry and retained data.** If the trial expires without valid lifetime access, creative editing, data package import, iCloud sync and the desktop character are paused. Data & Privacy remains available on the welcome screen and purchase notices, allowing free export of all or selected data packages, disabling sync, clearing diagnostic cache and reading policies, without first purchasing the App. Expiry does not automatically delete existing local or cloud data or change your rights in creative content. You may resume creation after obtaining valid access. Restoring purchases does not restore deleted content.

**Restoring purchases.** The App checks purchases automatically at launch. After reinstalling, moving to a supported Mac, or if access is not recognized, select “Already purchased?” in the purchase interface and use the App Store account that claimed the trial or made the purchase. Restoration does not charge you again. It restores only that account’s valid access; it does not extend the trial, reinstate refunded or revoked purchases, or recover deleted creative data. The App Store transaction account may differ from the system iCloud account. Restoring purchases is not a substitute for syncing or backing up creative data.

**Payment, cancellation and refunds.** Apple processes transactions. Cancelled, pending or unverified transactions do not immediately grant access. Refunds can be requested through [Apple’s refund service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/); eligibility and outcomes are determined by applicable law and Apple’s store rules. After receiving refund or revocation records, the App reassesses access against remaining valid purchase or trial records. If none remain, the features listed above are paused while existing data is retained. You may also contact the Author about transaction or usage issues.

**Offer codes.** If the Author provides an offer code, use “Redeem offer code” in purchase settings or the expiry notice to redeem it through Apple’s native interface. Apple validates the account, region, expiry, price and usage limits under the offer configuration. Apple’s confirmation sheet shows the actual price. A valid code may grant discounted or free lifetime access; the App grants access only after the resulting valid transaction is confirmed. The redemption deadline does not shorten lifetime access successfully obtained and does not create a recurring subscription. After a lifetime purchase is confirmed, the Purchase category is hidden in Settings.

## 4. Import, export and backups

Settings → Data Management lets you export all or selected data as a package, optionally including preferences. Dependencies are included automatically to preserve links. Character data includes saved speech rules, appearance adjustments, accessory states, part variants and outfits. Selecting a character may also include its world, linked records, images and parts. Review the actual scope before exporting.

Data package exports remain free after the trial ends and can be used for backups and later import into the App.

Import validates and merges data, keeping the local version when an internal ID already exists. Included preferences are restored after data import succeeds. Import merges data; it does not overwrite the library to restore a historical state. Items with the same name but different IDs may remain separate.

Exported packages and images may contain complete creative content and original file metadata. Packages include integrity checks but no App-level password encryption. Store them securely and use encrypted storage if needed. Other cloud services or recipients process exported files under the services you choose.

Keep independent backups before major edits, bulk deletion, import, device changes or software updates. Sync maintains consistency and does not replace an independent backup or permanent version history. The App does not promise to recover all accidentally deleted, overwritten or cleaned-up data.

## 5. iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. You can enable it directly in first-use feature settings or Settings → Data Management, or leave it off and continue using local features. The initial scope is all data. Once enabled in Data Management, the scope controls let you choose all or selected data. Required related content is included automatically; “All data” also includes future creations. Data is stored in the App’s private database for the current system iCloud account and is not published to other users.

The sync row explains private-account storage, that data is not sent to the developer or other servers, and the retention of existing copies. After reading it, you can enable sync directly without a confirmation dialog or checkbox declaration. The service region follows the system iCloud account and requires no manual selection. Account changes pause sync; actively enabling it again uses the current account. The interface does not collect age or guardian identity. Users under 14 must still be guided by a guardian under the Privacy Policy’s child-specific rules, including deciding whether to sync and obtaining consent where legally required. Keeping sync off does not affect local features. Recipients, regions, contacts and rights procedures are explained in Sections 8 and 10 of the Privacy Policy.

Turning sync off or reducing its scope retains existing local and cloud data; it does not delete cloud copies. Re-enabling sync or reselecting data resumes processing its changes. Account changes, network interruptions, service restrictions or insufficient storage may pause or delay sync.

Sync merges changes across devices. For conflicts that cannot be resolved automatically, you can choose the local or iCloud version. Deletions can also sync to other devices. Review content before choosing a conflict resolution or deleting it. Cloud data can be removed through Apple’s iCloud storage management. If other devices continue syncing, retained data may upload again. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Apple service availability, account rules and storage arrangements are governed by its applicable terms. The App will not ask for your Apple account password.

## 6. Local image processing, desktop characters and system features

Gallery previews, image processing, character part processing and illustration depth calculations run locally. Illustration depth uses a model bundled with the App and does not currently send images to external AI services. A result calculated from one image does not represent true 3D structure or exact physical distance.

Desktop character and illustration interactions use local pointer positions and necessary mouse events for dragging, clicking and visual effects. “Follow keyboard input” triggers actions from physical key positions and briefly displays keycap labels. Responding to other apps requires you to grant macOS Input Monitoring permission. You may restrict responses to this App or turn the feature off. The App does not save or upload keystroke logs or read composed input-method text. See the Privacy Policy for details.

You write character speech and configure its scenes, probabilities and intervals. Speech is displayed locally; it is not cloud AI conversation or an assessment of your actual mood, personality or behavior. Speech is creative content that can be saved, synced and exported with its character. Consider what is visible on your desktop or during screen sharing. You can hide the desktop character or adjust its settings. Launch at login is managed by macOS and can be disabled in the App or system login-item settings.

The App checks Apple’s public store version information and offers a link to the App Store. Apple handles downloading, installation and store transactions.
