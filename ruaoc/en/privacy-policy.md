# Rua OC Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the macOS app “布袋 OC”, known in English as “Rua OC” (the “App”), provided by Wu Guifu (吴贵福), pen name Futu (福涂), referred to as the “Author”, handles your data and personal information. Contact: [faurewu@icloud.com](mailto:faurewu@icloud.com).

**Core creative features run locally without an App account. iCloud sync is off by default. If enabled, selected data and required related content sync to the private database of the iCloud account currently signed in to macOS. Diagnostics stay on your device and are not automatically uploaded to the Author. Illustration depth calculations run locally without sending images to external AI services.**

“Stored locally” does not mean your data cannot contain personal information. Text, images, photo metadata and other assets may contain information about you or others. Choose what to import, sync and share based on your actual needs.

## 2. iCloud sync and Apple services

### 2.1 Optional sync

You can turn iCloud sync on or off directly in first-use feature settings or Settings → Data Management. You may leave it off and continue. The initial scope is all data; once enabled in Data Management, scope controls offer “All data” or “Select data”. Turning sync off retains the selected scope. Sync uses the iCloud account currently signed in to macOS. The App does not read or store your Apple account password.

Synced content includes more than item names: it includes the corresponding text, fields, original images, thumbnails, character parts and other saved data. To preserve references, selecting a character may include its world, linked records and images; selecting a world may include linked characters. Automatically included data is marked in the selection interface. “All data” includes existing and future creations.

Data is stored in the App’s private Apple CloudKit database for the current account, not a public database. The App does not publish it to other users or upload it to an Author-operated server. Sync manifests contain necessary internal IDs and relationship metadata. Reading a manifest alone does not download all full content outside the chosen scope.

Turning sync off or reducing its scope stops subsequent sync for the affected data but does not automatically delete existing local or cloud copies. The switch lets you withdraw your choice to use optional sync going forward; previously synced data must be deleted separately as described in Section 5. Turning the switch off does not retroactively undo completed processing.

First-use feature settings explain private-account storage and retained copies. The Data Management sync row explains that data goes to the current account’s private space, not to the developer or third-party servers outside iCloud, and that existing copies remain. You can enable the switch after reading this description without a confirmation dialog or a required or saved declaration. The service region is determined by the system iCloud account, without manual selection in the App. Opening Settings without enabling sync does not query or exchange cloud creative data. Users under 14 must use sync with guardian guidance and a guardian’s decision as described in Section 8.

Syncing real people’s health, identity, financial, movement or children’s information may involve sensitive personal information. Prefer fictional creative materials and avoid recording such information unnecessarily. If sync is necessary, its purpose is the cross-device storage and synchronization you choose; disclosure may harm privacy or property interests. Obtain separate consent where required by law. When handling others’ information, obtain appropriate authorization; an App user cannot give consent on behalf of a person beyond the user’s authority.

### 2.3 Service status and notifications

To sync, the App queries Apple for iCloud account availability, account identifiers and data changes, and uses system notifications and network-recovery events to trigger sync. Apple may process network, device or push information needed for connections. See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [iCloud terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/) for Apple’s own processing.

Actual iCloud storage locations, availability and rules depend on the account region and Apple’s services. This policy does not promise that all cloud data is in one country or region. The Author does not use the App to copy data to other overseas servers. Any processing requiring additional legal notice, authorization or other measures must meet those requirements; Apple’s terms do not replace them.

### 2.4 Checking for updates

The App automatically, or at your request, queries Apple’s public store lookup service at `itunes.apple.com` for the App’s released version. Requests contain the App’s store ID, store or system region code and software category, not creative data, diagnostics or Apple account passwords. Normal network requests also expose connection information such as IP addresses to the recipient service.

Results and check times are stored locally to reduce duplicate requests and display known updates offline. Selecting an update opens the App Store or an Apple product webpage. Apple handles actual downloads, installation and transactions.

### 2.5 Trial and in-app purchases

Claiming the trial, purchasing lifetime access and restoring purchases use Apple StoreKit. The App reads and verifies on-device Apple’s product IDs, transaction IDs, original purchase times and revocation status to validate purchases, calculate the consecutive 14-day trial, restore access and respond to refunds or revocations. Transaction information is used only for these functions, not advertising, profiling or cross-app tracking.

The App does not read your payment card number, billing address or Apple account password, ask you to enter them in the App, or send transaction records to an Author-operated server. Apple manages payment, account verification, transaction processing and fraud prevention under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and store terms. When connecting to Apple services, Apple may process network, device and account information needed to fulfill the request.

Purchases are checked when the App launches and becomes active, and StoreKit transaction updates are received while it runs. “Already purchased?” requests Apple to synchronize purchase records and may require a network connection and system account verification. Use the App Store account that claimed the trial or made the purchase. Restoration does not charge you again or extend the trial. This account may differ from the system iCloud account; the App does not merge creative data between them on that basis.

Offer codes are entered in Apple’s native redemption interface. Apple verifies eligibility, price and validity. The App does not read or store the entered code or use an Author-operated redemption service. It verifies the returned transaction using the fields described above to grant access. Cancellation, closing the redemption interface or failed redemption is not treated as a successful purchase.

Apple’s store services and system transaction history manage purchase records. The App processes information locally to determine access and does not put trial or purchase records into creative-data iCloud records, exported data packages or local diagnostics. Clearing the App’s cache, deleting creative data or uninstalling does not revoke purchases or guarantee deletion of transaction records retained by Apple. Retention and related rights requests follow Apple’s applicable rules.

If trial access expires or a transaction is revoked without other valid access, creative editing, import, iCloud sync and desktop characters pause. Local and cloud creative data is not automatically deleted. Data & Privacy on welcome or expiry screens still provides free package and readable JSON export, sync disabling, diagnostic cache cleanup and policy links. A purchase is not required to exercise these rights. Restoring purchases restores access, not creative content or backups. See the [User Agreement](user-agreement.md) for trial, purchase and expiry rules.

## 3. Local processing, system access, import and export

Gallery image processing, thumbnails, character part cropping and illustration depth calculations run locally. Depth uses a bundled Core ML model, does not send images to the model author or external AI platforms, and does not require downloading a model during use.

When you import files, the App reads the selected images or packages. Export writes your selected scope and necessary related content to your chosen location. Access follows macOS permissions and your actions. Features do not require contacts, precise location, camera or microphone data.

Data packages can include selected preferences such as theme, startup page, desktop character options including keyboard follow, document lock state and sync scope, restoring them after successful import. iCloud sync transfers creative data only, not these local preferences. Packages do not contain sync enablement, account binding, Input Monitoring or launch-at-login permissions, purchase access, diagnostic cache or onboarding progress. Language preference stays on each Mac and is not transferred by packages. Packages and exported original images have no App-level password encryption. Consider storage locations and recipients and use your chosen encryption tools if needed.

Desktop characters and illustrations use pointer positions or events locally, without recording or uploading other apps’ documents. Launch at login uses the macOS login-item mechanism and can be disabled in the App or System Settings.

“Follow keyboard input” uses physical key positions to trigger actions and briefly show labels on keycaps. It does not read text composed by an input method, save keystroke logs or upload them. Keycap labels disappear after about one second; the keyboard is put away after five seconds without typing. Keyboard follow is on by default; “Follow only in this app” is off by default. Responding to keys in other apps still requires you to grant macOS Input Monitoring permission. Without permission, only keys within this App trigger responses. If “Follow only in this app” is enabled, responses remain limited to this App even with permission. Responses pause while system Secure Input is active. Disable the feature in Desktop Character settings or revoke Input Monitoring in System Settings. Listening stops when the character is hidden.

You write character speech. The App chooses and displays it locally according to configured scenes, probabilities and intervals, without reading other apps’ chats or requesting AI-generated replies from the cloud. Speech text and rules are saved with the character and can be synced and exported. The current bubble, animation progress, pointer path and key events are not saved or synced as creative data. Speech can appear above other windows, so consider screen sharing and nearby viewers. Disable speech or hide the character when appropriate.

If you save exports to another cloud drive, email them or open them in another app, that service processes them under its rules. These are your subsequent choices. The App does not automatically send exported files to the Author.

## 4. Diagnostics and feedback

The App retains limited, sanitized local diagnostics to troubleshoot runtime errors, including code locations, error types and numeric codes, and necessary numeric fields and normalized stack locations from system-delivered crash reports.

Diagnostic caches and exports exclude creative text, images, user document paths, raw error descriptions, full original crash reports and passwords. Normal validation messages and cancellations are not logged as errors. Diagnostics are not automatically uploaded to the Author, and no third-party analytics are performed.

The cache retains up to 100 records from the last seven days, filtering expired entries when recording or reading. Disk cleanup does not depend on a scheduled task running after the App quits. You may clear it manually. Logs generated, retained or shared by macOS under system diagnostic settings are managed by the system and are not automatic App uploads.

Settings → About → Export diagnostics saves a text file that you may choose to email. For feedback you send, the Author uses only your contact address, issue description and necessary attachments to handle and answer the issue. General feedback emails are retained for at most six months after closure; raw troubleshooting attachments are deleted within 30 days after closure. Unrelated or mistakenly sent sensitive or children’s information is handled promptly when discovered and normally deleted within ten working days. You may request earlier deletion. If retention is legally required or necessary for an unresolved dispute, only necessary information is retained with restricted use and an explanation; it is deleted or de-identified when the period or purpose ends.

The feedback mailbox uses iCloud Mail. Transmission and storage are subject to the relevant email-service rules and may involve overseas processing. Do not send identity documents, passwords or unauthorized children’s or other sensitive information. If such information is necessary to resolve an issue, contact the Author first so the required scope, retention and authorization can be explained separately.

## 5. Retention and your choices

### 5.1 View, edit and export

You can view, edit and delete creative data in its respective pages, export all or selected data packages under Settings → Data Management, and export images from the gallery. For linked data, review the displayed dependencies and actual results.

Even after the trial ends or without valid purchase access, welcome-screen or expiry-screen Data & Privacy and the relevant Settings entry provide free export of all or selected data packages and a JSON copy readable and copyable in a text editor. Original binary files such as images remain complete as Base64. JSON is for reading, not App import. Contact the Author for help accessing, correcting, copying or deleting personal information. Handling rights requests does not require a purchase or submission of your entire library.

### 5.2 Delete data and manage copies

Creative data remains locally for continued use until you delete or clear the relevant library. Some deletions offer undo; complete any pending confirmation or undo period. Deleting data within an active sync scope may propagate to iCloud and other devices.

The App does not provide a single control to delete all local data. When creative features are available, you can delete individual items in their pages. After expiry, Data & Privacy provides free export, sync disabling and diagnostic cleanup. Contact the Author for help clearing a local library without providing the complete library.

Uninstalling alone does not guarantee deletion of local data, exports, system backups or cloud copies. Delete exports and your own backups separately. Copies retained by macOS or other backup services follow their management rules.

### 5.3 Turn off sync and delete cloud data

Turn off sync under Settings → Data Management. Reducing scope or turning sync off keeps existing cloud content.

To remove all of the App’s iCloud data, use macOS System Settings’ iCloud storage management according to Apple’s current controls. Turn off App sync on all relevant devices first; otherwise retained data on another device may upload again during later sync. Clearing cloud data does not delete local device copies, and vice versa.

Deleting individual cloud records requires completion of sync within the corresponding scope. For consistency and recovery, unreferenced cloud files have a grace period of at least 24 hours before later maintenance reclaims them. Removing an item from the interface does not mean every underlying copy is erased instantly. Apple’s backup and service retention rules apply separately.

### 5.4 Clear diagnostic cache and manage settings

Settings → General → Clear cache, also available through Data & Privacy after expiry, deletes the App’s current local diagnostic cache and corresponding in-memory records. It does not delete creative data, original images, parts or preferences. New errors may generate new local diagnostic records afterward.

Preferences and necessary sync state remain locally for later launches and recovery. Clearing diagnostic cache does not clear these settings. Do not treat sync recovery state as ordinary disposable cache.

### 5.5 Personal information rights and contact

You can access, edit, export, delete and stop optional syncing of the local and iCloud data you control through the functions above. The Author has no application backend to remotely view, modify or delete your local or private iCloud data.

For questions or requests to access, correct or delete feedback submitted to the Author, withdraw relevant consent or exercise other applicable legal rights, contact [faurewu@icloud.com](mailto:faurewu@icloud.com). The Author will respond within 15 working days with the outcome or necessary next steps, or sooner where law requires. Ordinary reasonable requests are free. Verification is limited to information necessary for the request; unrelated identity documents or a complete creative library are not required. If immediate deletion or another request cannot lawfully be fulfilled, the reasons, retention basis and applicable handling will be explained. You may also complain to a competent personal information protection authority or seek judicial remedies under law.

## 6. Disclosure and security

The App does not sell personal information, share creative data for advertising or marketing, or automatically provide local data or diagnostics to the Author. iCloud sync you enable, external links you open and files you send are handled as described above.

The App uses system storage and network services, a private cloud database, integrity checks and limited diagnostic fields to reduce risk. Libraries and packages do not have separate App-managed password encryption. Protect your data with macOS account security, FileVault, trusted backups and iCloud security settings. No measure guarantees zero risk.

If the Author receives feedback data, only people who need it to resolve the issue may use it within the necessary scope. Legally required disclosure will be checked for a valid basis and limited in scope. Personal information security incidents requiring notice will be handled and communicated as required by law.

## 8. Rules for children’s personal information

This section forms part of the policy and specifically applies to users under 14. Where it differs from general provisions, the stricter protection applies to children. Children should read it with a guardian, who should guide import, sync, export and external-service use. Users who are 14 or older but still minors must obtain guardian authorization where legally required, and transactions remain subject to Apple’s family and purchase rules.

**Scope and necessity.** The App does not actively request children’s real names, identity numbers, birth dates, schools, contact details or locations. Local creative work is handled under Section 1. Only if a guardian chooses sync are selected creative content, images, any children’s personal information within them and required identifiers synced through the recipients in Section 2 for cross-device storage and recovery. Avoid unnecessary real information about children. Children’s personal information is sensitive; disclosure may harm their privacy and safety.

**Guardian guidance and consent.** For users under 14, a guardian should read this section and the sync description and decide whether to enable sync. Obtain guardian consent as required by law for children’s information, including separate consent where required. The interface does not collect age, guardianship or sensitive-data declarations. Enabling sync does not prove age, guardianship or authorization to process others’ information. Leaving sync off does not affect local creation and requires no identity-document upload. iCloud’s age, family account and guardianship requirements still apply and cannot be bypassed through this App.

**Retention and security.** Retention, locations and deletion follow Sections 2, 4 and 5. Children’s data is not used for advertising, profiling, public display or AI training, sold because of App use, or supplied to marketers. The Author handles only feedback a guardian voluntarily submits and that is necessary to resolve an issue, without accessing private creative libraries through the App. Unauthorized or unnecessary children’s information is deleted promptly when discovered. Human access to feedback is limited to the people and scope needed for the issue.

**Withdrawal and rights.** Guardians may turn off sync at any time, export local data free of charge, and manage local, cloud and other-device data under Section 5. Contact [faurewu@icloud.com](mailto:faurewu@icloud.com) to request access, correction, copies or deletion of children’s feedback received by the Author, withdraw consent or complain. The Author responds within Section 5’s time limits and verifies only guardianship and information necessary for the request, without unrelated documents. If purposes, methods, information categories or recipients change and renewed consent is legally required, the guardian will be informed again and appropriate consent obtained.
